Closet App — a personal wardrobe manager for Špela at https://closet.pelamarela.com.


---
Wardrobe
Catalogue of all your clothing items. Add via photo — single photo goes straight to an AI-pre-filled form, multiple photos go to batch upload. Items have category, colour, warmth, formality, brand, material. 

Archive
items you no longer wear; restore them from the archived page.

Outfit log
Log outfits you actually wore — pick items, set the occasion, date, rating, and notes. Outfit history lives in a calendar-style library with wear counts per item.
  
AI Suggest
Get 3 outfit suggestions based on today's weather (auto-fetched, no key needed), a chosen occasion, and your style profile. Optionally anchor suggestions around a specific item you want to wear. Thumbs up/down feedback trains future suggestions. Regenerate to get fresh combinations.

Ideas
Save AI-suggested outfits as ideas for later. Browse, edit, and log them as real outfits when the time comes.

Shop
Paste a link or describe a piece you're considering buying. AI analyses how well it fits your existing wardrobe and style — gives a match score and honest reasoning.
  
Style profile
Free-text description of your style fed directly into AI prompts. Write it manually or generate it automatically from your outfit history.
